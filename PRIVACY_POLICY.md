# Privacy Policy — Pulse Music Player

**Last updated:** July 2026

## Data Collection

Pulse Music Player uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads.

## What We Do NOT Collect

- Your music library contents
- Your listening history (stored locally only)
- Any personal information
- Contacts, photos, or other sensitive data

## Local Storage

All data (playlists, favorites, settings, equalizer presets) is stored locally on your device only.

## AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your Google account settings.
